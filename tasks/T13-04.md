# T13-04  Security screen: frameworks, tier, rollout and exceptions

## Inputs
- SPEC §18–§21, §23
- policy catalogue through engine/internal/policy (T14-01)
- capabilities from discovery (T13-02)

## Outputs
- engine/internal/tui: Security step screens (frameworks, tier, rollout, settings by area, exceptions)
- draft `policy` section and `tenant.break_glass_accounts`

## Steps
1. Frameworks: one tick-box per framework in the catalogue, each with its coverage for the tenant's plan from `box policy coverage`; all five ticked by default.
2. Tier: `core` (default) or `enhanced`, showing the settings `enhanced` adds and what users notice.
3. Rollout: stage `audit` (default) or `enforce`, audit days (default 14), pilot group (default `grp-admins`, chosen from the configured groups).
4. Settings by area with their plain-language text from the catalogue, the requirements each serves, and its mode. Settings not available on the plan, and every `addon` setting, are shown as not applied with the add-on that would enable them.
5. Exceptions editor: choose a setting or requirement; reason, reference and end date are required, and the end date must be in the future. Every exception is listed on the Review screen (T13-07).
6. Fixed rules of SPEC §20.2 shown as locked; break-glass account names.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- An exception without a reason, a reference or a future end date is refused.
- A draft whose exception has expired is flagged on this screen and fails `box config validate`.
- The settings shown as applied equal `box policy resolve` for the same selection and plan.
- No add-on setting can be switched on.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
