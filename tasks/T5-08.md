# T5-08  Reminders, expiry and external paper (F10, F13)

## Inputs
- configuration `legal`

## Outputs
- flows F10, F13

## Steps
1. F10 daily: unsigned reminders; expiry notices to the legal mailbox; EXPIRED for lapsed unsigned documents; fallback reassignment of approvals.
2. F13: a file in External paper creates an EXTERNAL_PAPER item and always starts approval.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- Each reminder fires once on its day in a test.
- External paper cannot reach READY without approval.
