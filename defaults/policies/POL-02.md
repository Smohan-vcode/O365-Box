# Acceptable use policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose and scope

These are the rules everyone follows when using {{dictionary.company.trading_name}}'s accounts, devices, data and services. They apply to employees, directors, contractors and anyone else given a company account. You acknowledge them on joining and each time a new version is published.

## 2. Your account

- Your account is yours alone. Never share your password, your sign-in approvals or your authenticator device.
- Approve a sign-in request only when you have just tried to sign in yourself. If you get a request you did not expect, deny it and report it.
- Use a password you use nowhere else. A password manager approved by the company makes this easy.
- Do not try to get around sign-in checks, device checks or other security settings.

## 3. Devices

- Reach company data only from a device enrolled in company management or, on a personal phone, only through the protected company apps.
- Keep the screen lock on, storage encrypted and software up to date. Do not unlock (jailbreak or root) a phone that reaches company data.
- You are not an administrator on company computers; ask for the software you need.
- Write company data to removable storage only when the storage is encrypted and the company allows it.
- Report a lost or stolen device at once to {{generated.mailbox.security}}. The company may wipe company data from it, or the whole device if it is company-owned.
- Company devices are listed in an inventory and returned when you leave.

## 4. Email, chat and files

- Treat unexpected links, attachments, payment changes and urgent requests with suspicion. Use the Report button on any suspicious message.
- Do not forward company mail automatically to outside addresses, and do not send company files to personal mail accounts. If you must, you will be asked for a reason and the transfer is recorded.
- Share files outside the company only with named people, and only what they need (POL-05).
- Keep company information in company storage, not on personal cloud services.

## 5. Apps and AI

Use only the apps and AI features the company has approved (POL-21). Do not grant an outside app access to your company account; ask the owner instead.

## 6. Personal use

Limited personal use of company devices is allowed if it does not interfere with work, break the law or these rules, or put company data at risk. Activity on company systems is logged for security (POL-18); do not expect it to be private.

## 7. Not allowed

- Accessing information you have no business reason to see.
- Installing or running software meant to bypass security, capture data or attack systems.
- Using company services for unlawful, harassing or discriminatory content.
- Copying company data to a personal device or account, including when you leave.

## 8. Reporting

Report anything that looks wrong, including your own mistakes, to {{generated.mailbox.security}} or to the owner, {{dictionary.role.owner.name}}. Prompt reporting is always the right thing to do and is never held against you.
