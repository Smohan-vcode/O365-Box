# T2-04  Probe test and DMARC staging

## Inputs
- configuration `mail` (recommended objects in defaults/mail.yaml)

## Outputs
- command: box probe mail
- dated setup steps

## Steps
1. Send a tokenised message to every address and alias; confirm arrival in the right mailbox or member inbox.
2. From an external test mailbox, confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
3. Register dated steps: DMARC quarantine and MTA-STS enforce at day 14; DMARC reject at day 45.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- Every address delivers.
- Authentication passes for all three.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
