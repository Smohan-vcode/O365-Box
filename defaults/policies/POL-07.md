# Records retention policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose

{{dictionary.company.trading_name}} keeps records for as long as the law and the business need them, and no longer. This policy sets the schedule and how records are kept and deleted.

## 2. Scope

All records the company creates or receives, in any format: contracts, accounting and tax records, personnel files, correspondence, mail, chat, recordings and system logs.

## 3. Schedule

The schedule below comes from the company's configuration and the rules of the places where it operates ({{jurisdictions.operating}}). Each period starts at the event named in its row.

{{generated.retention_schedule}}

Where a law requires a longer period than the schedule, the longer period applies. The schedule is reviewed with this policy every year and whenever the company starts operating in a new place. The periods are a starting point, not legal advice; confirm them with an adviser.

## 4. Rules

1. Records are kept in the site and library the schedule names, never only on a personal device.
2. At the end of its period a record is deleted or, where the schedule says so, reviewed first.
3. Records that may be needed for a dispute, investigation or regulator request are placed on legal hold by the owner. A record on hold is not deleted until the hold is lifted.
4. Personal data is not kept longer than its purpose requires (POL-12).
5. Executed documents are never changed. Corrections are made by a new document.

## 5. How retention is applied

Where the subscription includes automatic retention, the schedule is applied as retention labels and policies. Where it does not, the owner reviews the records against the schedule each year as part of the policy review, and the result is recorded.

## 6. Roles

The owner approves the schedule and places and lifts legal holds. Site owners make sure records are filed in the right place.
