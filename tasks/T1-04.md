# T1-04  Device enrolment prerequisites

## Inputs
- resolved settings for the areas `intune-windows`, `intune-macos` and `intune-mobile` (SPEC §23); baseline/devices.yaml until T14-04 moves this step

## Outputs
- command: box apply devices

## Steps
1. Create compliance policies per platform and the app protection policy; assign to a dynamic or assigned group of users whose plan includes device management.
2. Configure automatic onboarding to the endpoint security service.
3. These are the prerequisites only; T14-05 to T14-07 add the remaining profiles per platform and own them from then on.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- Policies exist and are assigned only to eligible users.
- A test device reports compliant or lists the failing setting.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
