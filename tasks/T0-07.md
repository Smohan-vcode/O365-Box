# T0-07  Permission bundles granted by the selection

## Inputs
- SPEC §18.5, §24.3
- expected-state/app-permissions.yaml
- docs/spec-changes.md SC-21, SC-24

## Outputs
- expected-state/app-permissions.yaml grouped into bundles (base, devices, purview, powerplatform, teams, azure, …) with the settings and choices that need each
- bootstrap grants base plus the bundles the configuration needs; `box check identity` compares with that union
- Review screen data: bundles, why, and the settings that need them

## Steps
1. Group the current permissions into `base` and area bundles; add the SC-21 needs as bundles, each naming the settings that use it.
2. Compute the needed bundles from modules, choices and resolved settings.
3. Bootstrap grants exactly that union; a choice that adds a bundle later raises a gate for the Global Administrator sign-in.
4. `box check identity` fails on a permission outside the union, or a missing one.

## Human steps
- Approve the bundles at the bootstrap sign-in.

## Exit criteria
- A configuration without the devices module grants no device permission.
- Adding a choice that needs a bundle shows it on the Review screen and asks for the sign-in.
- `box check identity` passes on the test tenant for its selection.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).

## Status notes
- Engine done: expected-state/app-permissions.yaml holds the bundles base, devices (module devices),
  directory, app-consent (high privilege), teams, purview, security, powerplatform and azure.
  A bundle is needed by its module, a choice's effects, or an applied setting's mapping permissions
  or roles (resolved mappings only: a gate's roles are the person's). Settings whose provider or
  bundle belongs to a module that is off are left out ("module devices is off").
- `box choices permissions --config` lists the bundles with why and the settings (Review screen
  data, `--json`); `box bootstrap` prints the same list before the sign-in and grants exactly the
  union; `box check identity` compares the app with the union and says to re-run bootstrap when a
  bundle is missing.
- ExchangeOnlineManagement pinned at 3.9.2 (Connect-IPPSSession -AccessToken).
