# Use of AI policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose

AI tools can save time, but they can also leak confidential information, produce wrong answers and create legal risk. This policy sets how {{dictionary.company.trading_name}} uses them.

## 2. Approved tools

1. Use only the AI features and apps the company has approved. The approved list is kept with the allowed-apps list and published with this policy: {{generated.approved_ai}}.
2. Do not sign in to an AI service with your company account, or paste company information into one, unless it is on the approved list.
3. Users cannot grant outside apps access to company data or buy or trial services against the tenant. Ask the owner to add a tool.
4. AI features in automation tools are off unless the owner has approved them for a named purpose. Automation flows can connect only approved business services.

## 3. Using AI well

- **Information.** Give an approved AI tool only the information the task needs. Never give it Restricted information, credentials or special category personal data.
- **Check the output.** You are responsible for anything you send, publish or decide using AI. Check facts, figures, references and code before relying on them.
- **Be open.** Say when content shown to customers is substantially generated by AI, where the law or the customer's contract requires it.
- **People decisions.** Do not use AI to make decisions about people (hiring, performance, credit) without the owner's approval and a human review of each decision.
- **Intellectual property.** Do not ask AI tools to reproduce others' protected work, and check the tool's terms on ownership of output.

## 4. Review

Applications and AI tools with access to company data are listed and reviewed every month (PRC-13). A tool that is no longer needed or no longer meets this policy is removed.
