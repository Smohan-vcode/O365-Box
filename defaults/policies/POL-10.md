# Meeting recording and notes policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose

Recordings, transcripts and notes of meetings can hold personal and confidential information. This policy sets when meetings may be recorded and how the results are kept.

## 2. Recording

1. Record a meeting only when there is a business reason: training, an agreed record of a decision, or a participant who cannot attend.
2. Tell everyone at the start that the meeting is recorded. The meeting system shows a recording notice and asks participants to consent where the settings require it.
3. Do not record meetings about a person's health, discipline, grievance or performance unless the people involved agree in advance and the owner approves.
4. Do not record calls with outside parties without telling them first.

## 3. Outside participants

Outside participants wait in the lobby until admitted. Chat and calls with unmanaged outside accounts are off.

## 4. Notes and transcripts

- Notes and transcripts are company records. Keep them in the site of the team or matter, labelled at the right level (POL-05).
- Correct mistakes in notes promptly. Do not keep private copies.

## 5. Keeping and deleting

Recordings and transcripts are kept only as long as the retention schedule allows (POL-07), then deleted. Anyone in a recording may ask for a copy or for it to be deleted; handle such a request as POL-12 describes.
