# T9-01  Policy templates and rendering

## Inputs
- defaults/policies.yaml

## Outputs
- defaults/policies/*.md
- command: box render policies

## Steps
1. Write each policy as a Markdown template of at most two pages with placeholders only; front matter with id, version, owner role, review months, enforcement.
2. Enforcement references a path in baseline/ or a flow id, or `manual`.
3. Render with company values from the `Dictionary` to the Compliance site's `Policies` library as drafts; never overwrite a version edited there.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- All templates pass check-literals.
- Every policy names a baseline path, a flow id or `manual`.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
