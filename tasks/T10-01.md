# T10-01  Controls and evidence collectors

## Inputs
- defaults/controls.yaml

## Outputs
- engine/cli/collectors/
- weekly and monthly pipeline jobs

## Steps
1. Seed Controls. One collector per automated source: users and MFA registration, administrator role holders, Conditional Access policy export, site and library permissions, app consents, mail authentication, pull requests, drift report, sign-in and audit log export, acknowledgements, checklists, approvals.
2. Each writes a dated file to Compliance/Evidence and one Evidence item; no duplicate within a period.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- One artefact and one Evidence item per automated control after a run.
- A control without evidence this period shows as stale in its view.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
