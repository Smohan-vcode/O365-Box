# Contract and document management policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose

Contracts and other legal documents bind {{dictionary.company.legal_name}}. This policy makes sure each one is prepared from an approved template, approved by the right person, signed by someone with authority, and kept safely.

## 2. Scope

Every document the company sends or signs that creates, changes or ends a legal obligation: customer and supplier agreements, non-disclosure agreements, orders, employment and contractor documents, notices and letters with legal effect.

## 3. Rules

1. **Start from a template.** Create legal documents in the Documents register from the company's approved templates. The register gives each one a reference and fills in company details.
2. **Approval before sending.** A document is sent only after approval under the recorded rules. Changes to the company's own templates, and any paper received from the other side ("external paper"), go to the role the rules name. Nobody approves their own document when another approver exists.
3. **Authority to sign.** Only people listed in the company's signing authority may sign, within their limits.
4. **One register.** Every legal document has an item in the Documents register showing its status: draft, in review, ready, sent, executed, expired.
5. **Executed copies.** Signed copies are saved to the executed inbox, matched to their reference, moved to the counterparty folder and made read-only. An executed document is never changed; an amendment is a new document.
6. **No sharing out of the records sites.** Legal records cannot be shared outside the company directly from their sites. Send a copy through the approved process.
7. **Dates.** Renewal, expiry and notice dates are recorded so that reminders are raised in time.

## 4. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Sender | Prepares documents from templates and sends them once approved. |
| Legal approver, company legal administrator | Approve documents under the approval rules. |
| Signer | Signs within the signing authority. |
| Owner | Approves templates and the approval rules. |

## 5. Retention

Contracts are kept for the period in the retention schedule (POL-07), counted from the end of the contract.
