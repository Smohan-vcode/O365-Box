# Privacy and data-subject requests policy

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}} · Version {{generated.version}} · Approved: {{generated.date}} · Next review {{generated.next_review}}

## 1. Purpose

{{dictionary.company.legal_name}}, of {{dictionary.company.registered_address}}, handles personal data about customers, suppliers, staff and others. This policy sets out how it does so lawfully and how it answers people who ask about their data. It does not replace legal advice on the privacy laws of the places where the company operates ({{jurisdictions.operating}}).

## 2. Principles

Personal data is:

1. collected for a stated purpose and used only for that purpose or one compatible with it;
2. limited to what the purpose needs;
3. accurate and kept up to date;
4. kept no longer than the retention schedule allows (POL-07);
5. protected as Confidential or Restricted information (POL-05);
6. shared with suppliers only under an agreement (POL-11).

## 3. Requests from individuals

People may ask to see, correct, delete, restrict, move or object to the use of their personal data, as the law that applies to them allows.

1. Requests may come by any route. Anyone who receives one forwards it at once to {{generated.mailbox.privacy}}.
2. Each request becomes an item in the PrivacyRequests register with a due date set from the deadline the applicable law gives (commonly one month). A reminder is raised before the due date.
3. Confirm the person's identity in proportion to the request, without asking for more data than needed.
4. Search the sites, mailboxes and registers where the person's data may be held. Record what was searched.
5. Reply in plain language by the due date, or explain the extension the law allows and why.
6. Record the outcome and the date of the reply in the register.

## 4. Personal data leaving the company

Personal data sent outside the company is detected, and bulk transfers are blocked unless the sender gives a reason. Transfers to other countries follow the safeguards the applicable law requires.

## 5. Breaches

A loss or exposure of personal data is an incident. Report it at once to {{generated.mailbox.security}}; PRC-01 and PRC-02 apply, including any deadline to notify a regulator or the people affected.

## 6. Notices

The company's privacy notices are published from the legal pack for each place it operates and reviewed when processing changes.
