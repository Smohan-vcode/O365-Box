# T13-10  Choices screens

## Inputs
- SPEC §18.4, §24
- defaults/choices.yaml
- discovery (§18.3)

## Outputs
- choice screens in steps 3, 4 and 5; costs and bundles on the Review screen; 'Change a choice' on the home screen

## Steps
1. A reusable choice screen: question, help, options with detail and cost, recommended option preselected, detected values tagged.
2. Hide choices whose `when` does not hold; pre-fill from `detect`.
3. Review screen: permission bundles with the settings that need them, cost notes, gates added by choices.
4. Home screen: change a choice, then plan and apply the difference.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- Every choice renders at 80x24 in every theme.
- Accepting every recommendation reproduces the defaults the engine uses headless.
- A choice that adds a bundle is listed on the Review screen before the build.

## Evidence
- Engine: front matter `status: DONE` with the commit reference. Tenant: the step's `Setup` list item and evidence artefact (SPEC §14).
